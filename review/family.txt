## L1 Sisters, siblings, and family
  RULE Phîi and nɔ́ɔng: In Thai, brothers and sisters are always identified as either older or younger. Phîi is the prefix for "older" and nɔ́ɔng is for "younger". Phîi and nɔ́ɔng can also be used alone for "he/she" and "you", and phîi can be put before the names of older peers to show that they're older and respected.
  CHOICE พ่อแม่ | phɔ̂ɔ-mɛ̂ɛ: Siblings. / *Parents. / Younger brother. / Family.
  NEW พี่สาว | phîi-sǎao | older sister
  CHOICE พี่สาว | phîi-sǎao: Older brother. / *Older sister. / Younger sister. / Father.
  SPEAK พี่สาว | phîi-sǎao | older sister
  NEW น้องสาว | nɔ́ɔng-sǎao | younger sister
  CHOICE น้องสาว | nɔ́ɔng-sǎao: *Younger sister. / Younger brother. / Mother. / Older sister.
  BUILD "older sister, younger sister" = พี่สาว(phîi-sǎao) น้องสาว(nɔ́ɔng-sǎao)  [extra: nɔ́ɔng-chaai, phɔ̂ɔ]
  SPEAK น้องสาว | nɔ́ɔng-sǎao | younger sister
  NEW พี่น้อง | phîi-nɔ́ɔng | brothers and sisters / siblings
  CHOICE พี่น้อง | phîi-nɔ́ɔng: Family. / Younger sister. / *Brothers and sisters / siblings. / Parents.
  NEW ครอบครัว | khrɔ̂ɔp-khrua | family
  CHOICE ครอบครัว | khrɔ̂ɔp-khrua: Mother. / Older brother. / Siblings. / *Family.
  SPEAK ครอบครัว | khrɔ̂ɔp-khrua | family
  NEW กับ | gàp | and / with
  CHOICE พี่ชายกับน้องสาว | phîi-chaai gàp nɔ́ɔng-sǎao: *Older brother and younger sister. / Parents. / Older sister and younger brother. / Family.
  BUILD "family and siblings" = ครอบครัว(khrɔ̂ɔp-khrua) กับ(gàp) พี่น้อง(phîi-nɔ́ɔng)  [extra: phîi-sǎao, nɔ́ɔng-sǎao]
  SPEAK พี่น้องกับครอบครัว | phîi-nɔ́ɔng gàp khrɔ̂ɔp-khrua | Siblings and family.
  MATCH พี่สาว=older sister, น้องสาว=younger sister, พี่น้อง=brothers and sisters / siblings, ครอบครัว=family, กับ=and / with

## L6 Home, grandma, and grandpa
  RULE One big family: Some relationship terms are used to refer to people you're not related to — in Thai culture everyone is considered a big family. Yaai and taa are used for a woman/man your grandmother's/grandfather's age (they literally refer to your mother's mother/father), and lung, pâa, and náa are used the same way for an uncle or aunt's age.
  NEW ที่นี่ | thîi-nîi | here
  SPEAK ลูกอยู่ที่นี่ | lûuk yùu thîi-nîi | The children are here.
  BUILD "My younger brother is here." = น้องชาย(nɔ́ɔng-chaai) อยู่(yùu) ที่นี่(thîi-nîi)  [extra: sìi, khun]
  NEW บ้าน | bâan | home / house
  CHOICE พ่อแม่อยู่บ้าน | phɔ̂ɔ mɛ̂ɛ yùu bâan: Grandma is at home. / *My parents are at home. / The children are here. / Where is grandpa?
  NEW ยาย | yaai | grandmother (lit. mother's mother)
  CHOICE ยายอยู่บ้าน | yaai yùu bâan: Where is grandpa? / *Grandma is at home. / The children are here. / My parents are at home.
  SPEAK ยายอยู่บ้าน | yaai yùu bâan | Grandma is at home.
  NEW ตา | taa | grandfather (lit. mother's father)
  BUILD "Where is grandpa?" = ตา(taa) อยู่(yùu) ที่ไหน(thîi-nǎi)  [extra: nɔ́ɔng-chaai, kon]
  CHAT M:ตา อยู่ ที่ไหน(taa yùu thîi-nǎi=Where is grandpa?) → reply as F "Grandpa is at home.": มีลูกสามคน(mii lûuk sǎam kon) / *ตาอยู่บ้าน(taa yùu bâan) / ยาย(yaai)
  MATCH ที่นี่=here, บ้าน=home / house, ยาย=grandmother (lit. mother's mother), ตา=grandfather (lit. mother's father)

## L8 Married yet? Already or not
  RULE Married yet?: Marriage is considered inevitable in Thai, so instead of asking "Are you married?" people ask "Are you married yet?" using rɯ́-yang at the end of the question.
  NEW แต่งงาน | tɛ̀ng-ngaan | to marry
  NEW รึยัง | rɯ́-yang | ...yet?
  CHOICE คุณแต่งงานรึยัง | khun tɛ̀ng-ngaan rɯ́-yang: Yes, I'm married. / *Are you married yet? / Do you have a partner yet? / Do you have children yet?
  CHOICE มีแฟนรึยัง | mii fɛɛn rɯ́-yang: Do you have children yet? / *Do you have a partner yet? / Are you married yet? / Yes, I'm married.
  SPEAK มีลูกรึยัง | mii lûuk rɯ́-yang | Do you have children yet?
  SPEAK คุณแต่งงานรึยัง | khun tɛ̀ng-ngaan rɯ́-yang | Are you married yet?
  NEW แล้ว | láew | already
  BUILD "Yes, I'm married." = แต่งงาน(tɛ̀ng-ngaan) แล้ว(láew)  [extra: nɯ̀ng, yaai]
  BUILD "I already have children." = มี(mii) ลูก(lûuk) แล้ว(láew)  [extra: phîi-chaai, phɔ̂ɔ]
  CHAT M:คุณ แต่งงาน รึยัง(khun tɛ̀ng-ngaan rɯ́-yang=Are you married yet?) → reply as F "Yes, I'm married.": มีแฟนรึยัง(mii fɛɛn rɯ́-yang) / *แต่งงานแล้ว(tɛ̀ng-ngaan láew) / ลุง(lung)
  NEW ยัง | yang | yet / still
  MATCH แต่งงาน=to marry, รึยัง=...yet?, แล้ว=already, ยัง=yet / still
