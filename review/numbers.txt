## L1 Nine to nineteen

  NEW เก้า | kâo | nine
  NEW สิบ | sìp | ten

## L2 Tens and two-digit numbers
  RULE Tens: unit + sip (except twenty): For 20, 30, 40 and so on, put the unit number before sìp — sǎam-sìp is 30. "Twenty" is the exception: it's yîi-sìp, not sɔ̌ɔng-sìp.
  BUILD "Forty, fifty" = สี่(sìi) สิบ(sìp) ห้า(hâa) สิบ(sìp)  [extra: hòk, pɛ̀ɛt]
  CHOICE สี่สิบหกสิบ | sìi-sìp hòk-sìp: Fifty, sixty / Forty, fifty / Thirty, forty / *Forty, sixty
  BUILD "Seventy, eighty" = เจ็ด(jèt) สิบ(sìp) แปด(pɛ̀ɛt) สิบ(sìp)  [extra: kâo, nɯ̀ng]
  SPEAK แปดสิบเก้าสิบ | pɛ̀ɛt-sìp kâo-sìp | Eighty, ninety
  RULE Building any two-digit number: For other two-digit numbers, add the unit after the tens — "45" is sìi-sìp-hâa, "99" is kâo-sìp-kâo, and "21" is yîi-sìp-èt.
  CHOICE สี่สิบห้าหกสิบเจ็ด | sìi-sìp-hâa hòk-sìp-jèt: Forty-five, seventy-six / *Forty-five, sixty-seven / Sixty-seven, ninety-nine / Fifty-four, seventy-six
  BUILD "Ninety-nine" = เก้า(kâo) สิบ(sìp) เก้า(kâo)  [extra: pɛ̀ɛt, sǔun]
  CHOICE หกสิบเจ็ดแปดสิบสาม | hòk-sìp-jèt pɛ̀ɛt-sìp-sǎam: Seventy-six, eighty-three / Sixty-seven, ninety-nine / *Sixty-seven, eighty-three / Forty-five, sixty-seven
  SPEAK สี่สิบห้าหกสิบเจ็ดแปดสิบสาม | sìi-sìp-hâa hòk-sìp-jèt pɛ̀ɛt-sìp-sǎam | Forty-five, sixty-seven, eighty-three
  NEW ยี่สิบ | yîi-sìp | twenty
  CHOICE ยี่สิบสามสิบ | yîi-sìp sǎam-sìp: Forty, fifty / *Twenty, thirty / Ten, twenty / Thirty, forty
  BUILD "Twenty-one, twenty-two" = ยี่สิบ(yîi-sìp) เอ็ด(èt) ยี่สิบ(yîi-sìp) สอง(sɔ̌ɔng)  [extra: hâa, sǎam]

## L3 Hundreds and thousands
  RULE Hundreds: "One hundred" is rɔ́ɔi or nɯ̀ng-rɔ́ɔi. Add the tens and units after it — rɔ́ɔi-hâa-sìp is 150.
  RULE Thousands: "One thousand" is phan or nɯ̀ng-phan. Add the hundreds, tens and units after it — sɔ̌ɔng-phan-hâa-rɔ́ɔi is 2,500.
  NEW ร้อย | rɔ́ɔi | hundred
  CHOICE ร้อยสองร้อยสามร้อยสี่ร้อย | rɔ́ɔi sɔ̌ɔng-rɔ́ɔi sǎam-rɔ́ɔi sìi-rɔ́ɔi: One hundred, two hundred, three hundred / Two hundred, three hundred, four hundred, five hundred / *One hundred, two hundred, three hundred, four hundred / One hundred, three hundred, four hundred, five hundred
  BUILD "Four hundred fifty" = สี่(sìi) ร้อย(rɔ́ɔi) ห้า(hâa) สิบ(sìp)  [extra: nɯ̀ng, hòk]
  CHOICE ห้าร้อยหกร้อยเจ็ดร้อยแปดร้อย | hâa-rɔ́ɔi hòk-rɔ́ɔi jèt-rɔ́ɔi pɛ̀ɛt-rɔ́ɔi: Five hundred, six hundred, seven hundred / Six hundred, seven hundred, eight hundred, nine hundred / Five hundred, seven hundred, eight hundred, nine hundred / *Five hundred, six hundred, seven hundred, eight hundred
  BUILD "Six hundred fifty-five" = หก(hòk) ร้อย(rɔ́ɔi) ห้า(hâa) สิบ(sìp) ห้า(hâa)  [extra: sɔ̌ɔng, sǎam]
  SPEAK ร้อยสองร้อยสามร้อย | rɔ́ɔi sɔ̌ɔng-rɔ́ɔi sǎam-rɔ́ɔi | One hundred, two hundred, three hundred

  BUILD "Two thousand five hundred" = สอง(sɔ̌ɔng) พัน(phan) ห้า(hâa) ร้อย(rɔ́ɔi)  [extra: sìp, hòk]
  CHOICE ห้าพันหกพันเจ็ดพันแปดพัน | hâa-phan hòk-phan jèt-phan pɛ̀ɛt-phan: *Five thousand, six thousand, seven thousand, eight thousand / Five thousand, six thousand, seven thousand / Six thousand, seven thousand, eight thousand, nine thousand / Five thousand, seven thousand, eight thousand, nine thousand
  BUILD "Nine thousand nine hundred" = เก้า(kâo) พัน(phan) เก้า(kâo) ร้อย(rɔ́ɔi)  [extra: hòk, hâa]
  SPEAK หกพันเจ็ดพันแปดพันเก้าพัน | hòk-phan jèt-phan pɛ̀ɛt-phan kâo-phan | Six thousand, seven thousand, eight thousand, nine thousand
  MATCH ร้อย=hundred, พัน=thousand

## L5 Ordinal numbers and do you have it?
  RULE Ordinal numbers: Put thîi (falling tone) before a number to make an ordinal — "the third person" is kon thîi sǎam, "the second day" is wan thîi sɔ̌ɔng.
  NEW ที่ | thîi | ordinal marker ("-th")
  NEW คน | kon | person (classifier for people)
  CHOICE คนที่หนึ่ง | kon thîi nɯ̀ng: the second person / *the first person / There are five people. / the third person
  BUILD "the second person" = คน(kon) ที่(thîi) สอง(sɔ̌ɔng)  [extra: rɔ́ɔi, mɯ̀ɯn]
  SPEAK คนที่สาม | kon thîi sǎam | the third person
  SPEAK คนที่หนึ่ง | kon thîi nɯ̀ng | the first person
  NEW เอา | ao | want / take
  SPEAK เอาสอง | ao sɔ̌ɔng | I'll take two.
  NEW มี | mii | have
  CHOICE มีห้าคน | mii hâa kon: the first person / the second person / the third person / *There are five people.
  BUILD "There are three of us." = มี(mii) สาม(sǎam) คน(kon)  [extra: hâa, sìi]
  MATCH ที่=ordinal marker ("-th"), คน=person (classifier for people), เอา=want / take, มี=have

## L10 Telling time, morning to night
  RULE Telling time: mong: For 6am-11am, put mong after the number and add cháo ("morning") — hòk mong cháo is 6am. For 1pm-3pm, put bàai ("afternoon") first: bàai sɔ̌ɔng mong is 2pm. For 4pm-6pm, add yen ("evening") instead: sìi mong yen is 4pm.
  RULE Evening and early-morning hours: For 7pm-11pm, count 1-5 and add thûm: sɔ̌ɔng thûm is 8pm. For 1am-5am, put tii before the number: tii hâa is 5am. Thîang is noon.
  NEW โมง | mong | o'clock (daytime hour)
  CHOICE กี่โมง | gìi mong: 2 p.m. / 8 p.m. / *What time is it? / 6 a.m.
  SPEAK กี่โมง | gìi mong | What time is it?
  NEW เช้า | cháo | morning
  BUILD "6 a.m." = หก(hòk) โมง(mong) เช้า(cháo)  [extra: jèt, níi]
  NEW บ่าย | bàai | afternoon
  CHOICE บ่ายสองโมง | bàai sɔ̌ɔng mong: What time is it? / 6 a.m. / 8 p.m. / *2 p.m.
  CHAT M:กี่ โมง(gìi mong=What time is it?) → reply as F "3 p.m.": สองร้อยบาท(sɔ̌ɔng rɔ́ɔi bàat) / เบอร์โทรศัพท์(bəə thoo-rá-sàp) / *บ่ายสามโมง(bàai sǎam mong)
  NEW ทุ่ม | thûm | evening hour (7pm-11pm count)
  SPEAK สองทุ่ม | sɔ̌ɔng thûm | 8 p.m.
  NEW ตี | tii | early-morning hour (1am-5am count)
  BUILD "5 a.m." = ตี(tii) ห้า(hâa)  [extra: an, cháo]
  NEW เที่ยง | thîang | noon
  SPEAK เที่ยง | thîang | noon
  MATCH โมง=o'clock (daytime hour), เช้า=morning, บ่าย=afternoon, ทุ่ม=evening hour (7pm-11pm count), ตี=early-morning hour (1am-5am count), เที่ยง=noon
