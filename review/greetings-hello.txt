# greetings-hello — Hello & Everyday Phrases

## L0 Hello!
  RULE Polite particles: Thai has polite words added to the end of a question, response, or statement: khráp for men; for women, khâ after statements and answers and khá after questions. They make it sound more polite. They are used with people older than you, or whenever you want to sound polite — for example, when first meeting someone.
  NEW สวัสดี | sà-wàt-dii | hello
  NEW ค่ะ | khâ | polite particle (said by women)
  CHOICE สวัสดีค่ะ | sà-wàt-dii khâ: Good luck. / *Hello. (said by a woman) / Excuse me. / Thank you.
  SPEAK สวัสดีค่ะ | sà-wàt-dii khâ | Hello. (said by a woman)
  BUILD "Hello. (said by a woman)" = สวัสดี(sà-wàt-dii) ค่ะ(khâ)  [extra: ]
  CHOICE ค่ะ | khâ: Polite particle (said by a man). / Thank you. / *Polite particle (said by a woman). / Hello.
  NEW ครับ | khráp | polite particle (said by men)
  CHAT M:สวัสดี ครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp=Hello. (said by a man)) → reply as F "Hello. (said by a woman)": สวัสดีครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp) / *สวัสดีค่ะ(sà-wàt-dii khâ) / ครับ(khráp)
  CHOICE สวัสดีครับ | sà-wàt-dii khráp: How are you? / Thank you. / Good luck. / *Hello. (said by a man)
  SPEAK สวัสดีครับ | sà-wàt-dii khráp | Hello. (said by a man)
  CHOICE ครับ | khráp: *Polite particle (said by a man). / Polite particle (said by a woman). / Thank you. / Hello.
  NEW ไป | pai | go
  NEW ไหน | nǎi | where
  CHOICE ไปไหน | pai nǎi: Hello. / *Where are you going? / What's your name? / Good luck.
  BUILD "Where are you going?" = ไป(pai) ไหน(nǎi)  [extra: khráp, sà-wàt-dii]
  MATCH สวัสดี=hello, ค่ะ=polite particle (said by women), ครับ=polite particle (said by men), ไป=go, ไหน=where

## L2 Good, or not?
  RULE Yes/no questions with mái: To make a simple question like "is it good?", put mái (high tone) after the word. Answer "yes" by repeating the word, or "no" by putting mâi (falling tone) before it.
  NEW ดี | dii | good
  CHOICE ดี | dii: question particle: is it ___? / not / no / hello / *good
  NEW มา | maa | come (back) — pai nǎi maa: where have you been?
  NEW มั้ย | mái | question particle: is it ___?
  CHOICE ดีมั้ย | dii mái: Is it fun? / Hello. / *Is it good? / Where are you going?
  BUILD "Is it good?" = ดี(dii) มั้ย(mái)  [extra: hǎa, pai]
  SPEAK ดีมั้ย | dii mái | Is it good?
  NEW ไม่ | mâi | not / no
  CHOICE ไม่ดี | mâi dii: Is it good? / *No, it's not good. / Where are you going? / Yes, it's good.
  BUILD "No, it's not good." = ไม่(mâi) ดี(dii)  [extra: khâ, khráp]
  CHOICE ไม่ | mâi: hello / question particle: is it ___? / *not / no / good
  CHAT M:ไป ไหน มา(pai nǎi maa=Where have you been?) → F:ไป เที่ยว มา(pai thîao maa=I went out.) → M:ดี มั้ย(dii mái=Was it good?) → reply as F "Good.": ไม่ดี(mâi dii) / สวัสดีครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp) / ไปเที่ยว(pai thîao) / *ดี(dii)
  CHAT M:สวัสดี ครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp=Hello. (said by a man)) → F:สวัสดี ค่ะ(sà-wàt-dii khâ=Hello. (said by a woman)) → M:ไป เที่ยว มา ดี มั้ย(pai thîao maa, dii mái=Was your trip good?) → reply as F "No, it's not good.": ดี(dii) / ไปเที่ยว(pai thîao) / *ไม่ดี(mâi dii) / ไปไหน(pai nǎi)
  SPEAK ไม่ดี | mâi dii | No, it's not good.
  MATCH ดี=good, มา=come (back) — pai nǎi maa: where have you been?, มั้ย=question particle: is it ___?, ไม่=not / no
