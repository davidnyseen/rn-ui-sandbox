# greetings-hello — Hello & Everyday Phrases

## L0 Hello!
  RULE Polite particles: Thai has two words, khâ for women and khráp for men, added to the end of a question, response, or statement to make it sound more polite. They are used with people older than you, or whenever you want to sound polite — for example, when first meeting someone.
  NEW สวัสดี | sà-wàt-dii | hello
  NEW ค่ะ | khâ | polite particle (said by women)
  CHOICE สวัสดีค่ะ | sà-wàt-dii khâ: Good luck. / *Hello. (said by a woman) / Excuse me. / Thank you.
  SPEAK สวัสดีค่ะ | sà-wàt-dii khâ | Hello. (said by a woman)
  BUILD "Hello. (said by a woman)" = สวัสดี(sà-wàt-dii) ค่ะ(khâ)  [extra: ]
  CHOICE ค่ะ | khâ: Polite particle (said by a man). / Thank you. / *Polite particle (said by a woman). / Hello.
  NEW ครับ | khráp | polite particle (said by men)
  CHAT M:สวัสดี ครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp=Hello. (said by a man)) → reply as F "Hello. (said by a woman)": ค่ะ(khâ) / *สวัสดีค่ะ(sà-wàt-dii khâ) / ครับ(khráp)
  CHOICE สวัสดีครับ | sà-wàt-dii khráp: How are you? / Thank you. / Good luck. / *Hello. (said by a man)
  SPEAK สวัสดีครับ | sà-wàt-dii khráp | Hello. (said by a man)
  CHOICE ครับ | khráp: *Polite particle (said by a man). / Polite particle (said by a woman). / Thank you. / Hello.
  NEW ไป | pai | go
  NEW ไหน | nǎi | where
  CHOICE ไปไหน | pai nǎi: Hello. / *Where are you going? / What's your name? / Good luck.
  BUILD "Where are you going?" = ไป(pai) ไหน(nǎi)  [extra: khráp, sà-wàt-dii]
  MATCH สวัสดี=hello, ค่ะ=polite particle (said by women), ครับ=polite particle (said by men), ไป=go, ไหน=where

  CHAT M:สวัสดี ครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp=Hello. (said by a man)) → F:สวัสดี ค่ะ(sà-wàt-dii khâ=Hello. (said by a woman)) → M:ไป ไหน(pai nǎi=Where are you going?) → reply as F "I'm going out/traveling around.": *ไปเที่ยว(pai thîao) / สวัสดีค่ะ(sà-wàt-dii khâ) / ครับ(khráp)

## L2 Good, or not?
  RULE Yes/no questions with mái: To make a simple question like "is it good?", put mái (high tone) after the word. Answer "yes" by repeating the word, or "no" by putting mâi (falling tone) before it.
  NEW ดี | dii | good
  CHOICE ดี | dii: not / no / hello / *good / question particle: is it ___?
  NEW มั้ย | mái | question particle: is it ___?
  CHOICE ดีมั้ย | dii mái: Where are you going? / Is it fun? / Hello. / *Is it good?
  BUILD "Is it good?" = ดี(dii) มั้ย(mái)  [extra: phɯ̂an, thîao]
  SPEAK ดีมั้ย | dii mái | Is it good?
  NEW ไม่ | mâi | not / no
  CHOICE ไม่ดี | mâi dii: Where are you going? / *No, it's not good. / Is it good? / Yes, it's good.
  BUILD "No, it's not good." = ไม่(mâi) ดี(dii)  [extra: pai, nǎi]
  CHOICE ไม่ | mâi: question particle: is it ___? / *not / no / good / hello
  CHAT M:ไป ไหน(pai nǎi=Where did you go?) → F:ไป เที่ยว(pai thîao=I went out/traveled around.) → M:ดี มั้ย(dii mái=Was it good?) → reply as F "Good.": *ดี(dii) / ไปเที่ยว(pai thîao) / สวัสดีครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp) / ไม่ดี(mâi dii)
  CHAT M:สวัสดี ครับ(sà-wàt-dii khráp=Hello. (said by a man)) → F:สวัสดี ค่ะ(sà-wàt-dii khâ=Hello. (said by a woman)) → M:ดี มั้ย(dii mái=Is it good?) → reply as F "No, it's not good.": *ไม่ดี(mâi dii) / ไปไหน(pai nǎi) / ดี(dii) / ไปเที่ยว(pai thîao)
  SPEAK ไม่ดี | mâi dii | No, it's not good.
  MATCH ดี=good, มั้ย=question particle: is it ___?, ไม่=not / no
