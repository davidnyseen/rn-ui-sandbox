### General Native Advice for this Unit:

1.  **Politeness Particles:** In Thailand, when talking about family (a personal topic), you should almost always end your sentences with **ครับ (khráp)** if you are male or **ค่ะ (khâ)** if you are female. Without them, the learner sounds aggressive or robotic.
2.  **Addressing Elders:** As noted in the "Father" fix, avoid using "He/She" (*kháo*) for parents, aunts, or uncles. It is much more natural to repeat their title. Instead of "He is at home," say "Uncle is at home" (**ลุงอยู่บ้านครับ** - *Lung yùu bâan khráp*).
3.  **"Where":** While **อยู่ที่ไหน** (*yùu thîi-nǎi*) is correct, in fast everyday Bangkok speech, we often shorten it to **อยู่ไหน** (*yùu nǎi*). For example: **พ่ออยู่ไหน?** (*phɔ̂ɔ yùu nǎi?*). Both are acceptable, but the shorter version is very common.
