### General Note for this Unit:
1.  **Polite Particles (คะ vs ค่ะ):** This is the most important fix for beginner apps. 
    *   **คะ (khá - high tone)** is for questions: *สบายดีมั้ยคะ?* (Are you well?)
    *   **ค่ะ (khâ - falling tone)** is for statements and "yes": *สบายดีค่ะ* (I'm well.)
2.  **Parting Phrasing:** In English, we say "Thank you, goodbye." In Thai, we just say "I'll be going now" (**ไปก่อนนะ**) or "See you again" (**แล้วเจอกันใหม่**). Saying "Thank you" (**ขอบคุณ**) at the end of a simple greeting feels like you are thanking them for an interview or a business deal.
